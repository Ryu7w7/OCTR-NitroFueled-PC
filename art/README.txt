CTR Nitro-Fueled PC launcher UI artwork

ctr-hero.png: SteamGridDB hero art (asset 84321), displayed as a static image at its original color grade.
ctr-logo.png: SteamGridDB logo art (asset 89549), scaled proportionally over the hero image.

The generated background contains no game logo or characters. Game artwork remains the property of its respective owners.

ctr-menu.ttf: Lilita One display font for game-inspired menu labels and the primary Play button.
ctr-menu-font-license.txt: SIL Open Font License 1.1 for Lilita One.

ctr-text.ttf: CCGeekSpeak (Comicraft), the launcher's and in-game menu's text font (Tacho's menu redesign).
ctr-title.ttf: CCZoinks (Comicraft), the launcher's titles and Play button.
These two are commercial fonts: staff/test builds only until their license is cleared for public packages.
Without them the launcher and the in-game menu use ctr-menu.ttf and DejaVu as before.
ctr-overlay-background.png: the in-game menu's semi-transparent background (Tacho).
